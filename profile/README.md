## About GarageGo

GarageGo is a **smart vehicle service platform** that connects vehicle owners with verified garages in real-time.

It enables users to:
-  Find nearby garages instantly  
-  Book vehicle services online  
-  Make secure advance payments  
-  Track repair progress in real-time  
-  Request emergency roadside assistance  

Garage owners and mechanics get a complete digital system to manage:
- Bookings & scheduling  
- Service capacity & workflow  
- Earnings & analytics  
- Customer communication  

Admins ensure platform quality through:
- Garage verification & approval  
- Complaint handling  
- System monitoring & analytics
